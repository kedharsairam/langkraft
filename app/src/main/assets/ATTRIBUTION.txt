LangKraft — sources and attribution

Phrase content
==============
LingoHut (https://www.lingohut.com/) supplied the list of lesson topics and English meanings
used as anchors. No LingoHut text was copied into this app; their site terms prohibit copying
and public display. All target-language wording was authored for LangKraft.

Where a row's `source` field says "Wikivoyage: <Language> phrasebook (CC BY-SA 4.0)", that
wording was checked against the corresponding Wikivoyage phrasebook at the time of authoring
(https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/<Language>_phrasebook). Egyptian Arabic was checked against
https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Egyptian_Arabic_phrasebook. Rows marked "LangKraft authored"
were composed from those vocabularies and verified for register and current usage.

Wikivoyage phrasebooks used (all CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/):
  Spanish, Indonesian, Portuguese, Hindi, French, Arabic (Egyptian), Chinese (Mandarin),
  Swahili, Russian, Vietnamese, Thai, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, German, Italian, Persian
  (Farsi), Serbian, Tamil.

Every phrase and number row carries its own `source` and `checked` date in the JSONL assets.
The original research snapshots were kept outside this repository and are no longer available;
attribution above records what was consulted, not a re-verification of every row.

Note: "Wikivoyage" and "LangKraft authored" are provenance labels for human review, not a claim
that every phrase is a verbatim quote. CC BY-SA applies to derived wording; this app is offered
under the same licence for those rows.
